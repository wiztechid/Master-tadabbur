# TadabburLife — Private SEO + AdSense Publication Governance

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** PRE-RESUBMISSION GOVERNANCE  
**Authority:** subordinate to `mastersoptadabbur.md` and its **ABSOLUTE SACRED LOCK**.

## 1. Non-negotiable sacred boundary

No SEO, AdSense, keyword, thin-content, UX, translation, or monetization task may rewrite, paraphrase, optimize, shorten, expand, or silently correct:

- Qur'an Arabic text;
- surah/ayah identity or verified reference;
- the Qur'an translation already established for a session;
- quoted hadith text;
- verified hadith identity/reference/attribution.

If a suspected source error is found, stop the optimization on that block and route it through the existing **Verified Source Correction** process. Search demand never overrides religious-source integrity.

## 2. What may be expanded

To improve reader value, search-intent coverage, and AdSense content sufficiency, editors may add **original, session-specific explanatory value** outside the sacred blocks. Preferred expansion zones are:

1. **Context / konteks kehidupan** — explain the real reader problem and why the session matters.
2. **Interpretation / prediction boundary** — clearly separate the text/evidence from editorial reflection; prevent unsupported modern-event prediction, guaranteed outcomes, or forced religious claims.
3. **Muhasabah** — specific reflection questions tied to the session's meaning.
4. **Practical action** — realistic, reversible, proportionate actions traceable to the session's evidence and meaning.
5. **Journey relationship** — explain what this session adds compared with the previous/next/owner session.
6. Supporting answer blocks, examples, summaries, FAQ, and internal-link context only when they answer genuine search intent and add standalone reader value.

These additions must never invent a fatwa, prohibition, obligation, virtue, spiritual promise, causal religious claim, or prediction merely because a keyword is attractive.

## 3. Keyword Opportunity Gate

A keyword/search territory may influence explanatory content only when all are true:

**SEARCH OPPORTUNITY + SESSION RELEVANCE + INTENT FIT + ORIGINAL VALUE + RELIGIOUS-EVIDENCE SAFETY + CANNIBALIZATION SAFETY.**

Rules:

- ID and EN are researched independently; English is not merely a translated Indonesian keyword map.
- Do not target a keyword if answering it would require stretching the ayah/hadith meaning.
- Do not repeat keywords unnaturally.
- One primary intent territory has one canonical landing.
- Before adding a new target, compare against `seo/keyword-map.json`, active owner sessions, companion sessions, and related pages.
- Search opportunity may decide **which useful question to explain**, never **what revelation means**.

## 4. AdSense Content-Value Gate

Google may review pages beyond the submitted URL. Therefore every indexable session must be defensible as a useful destination, not merely a bridge to another page.

An indexable session must provide:

- a clear unique reader purpose;
- complete sentences and substantive explanatory content;
- original value beyond navigation, headings, counters, quotations, or links;
- enough standalone context that a search visitor understands why the page exists;
- clear navigation and a meaningful next step;
- no doorway/cookie-cutter behavior.

There is **no mechanical word-count quota**. Depth is judged by whether the page fully answers its intended reader need without filler.

### Companion / practical-checkpoint rule

A companion session must continue respecting **ONE AYAT → ONE PRIMARY OWNER**. It must not duplicate the owner's Qur'an block or tafsir merely to become longer.

Instead, make it independently useful through a distinct practical intent, e.g. a method, checklist, decision framework, reflection exercise, examples, failure modes, context, and journey relationship. If it still cannot justify a standalone search landing without duplicating the owner, review its indexability rather than manufacturing filler.

## 5. Thin / Borderline / Strong corpus classification

Before AdSense resubmission, audit every active **session × language**.

- **STRONG** — clear standalone purpose, substantive original explanation, search intent satisfied, sacred boundary intact, no cannibalization.
- **BORDERLINE** — useful but one or more explanatory layers are too shallow, generic, repetitive, or dependent on another page.
- **THIN / P0** — mostly quotation/navigation/template/redirect-like content; insufficient standalone value; duplicated owner material; or content that exists primarily to funnel users elsewhere.

Any THIN/P0 indexable session blocks AdSense resubmission until it is either strengthened within this governance or intentionally removed from search inventory through the existing indexability process.

## 6. Mass-produced / template-laundering guard

Shared design is allowed; shared substance is not.

Fail the publication gate when multiple pages differ mainly by title, verse reference, keyword substitutions, or mechanically translated prose. Each session must contain session-specific reasoning/value appropriate to its topic.

Do not inflate pages with generic introductions, repeated moral advice, duplicated FAQ, boilerplate conclusions, or keyword variants solely to increase length.

## 7. ID ↔ EN governance

Sacred anchor ownership and religious meaning remain aligned across languages. Explanatory SEO does not need 1:1 structural parity.

An English page may use different examples, questions, headings, FAQ, or contextual explanation when English-language search intent supports it, provided the religious anchor and evidence boundary remain consistent.

## 8. AdSense technical pre-resubmission gate

Before resubmission verify on **live production**, not repository source alone:

- AdSense account/site connection method is actually present and detectable where required;
- correct publisher ID;
- `ads.txt` live and correct;
- robots/canonical/hreflang/sitemap/indexability coherent;
- public pages HTTP 200 and crawler-readable;
- no accidental noindex on intended search landings;
- About, Contact, Privacy, Cookies, Terms, Disclaimer remain reachable;
- ad implementation does not obscure content, imitate navigation, encourage clicks, or sit deceptively near interactive controls;
- consent implementation matches the production advertising configuration where required.

A repository PASS or successful GitHub Pages build is not enough: verify **source → artifact → live custom domain**.

## 9. Pre-resubmission release sequence

1. Freeze sacred-source baseline/hash or equivalent comparison evidence.
2. Run 94-page ID/EN content-value audit.
3. Resolve THIN/P0 without duplicating owner sacred content.
4. Resolve material BORDERLINE pages where search value remains weak.
5. Strengthen homepage/publisher explanation if crawler-visible value is insufficient.
6. Verify AdSense technical connection and live ads.txt.
7. Run Sacred Source Integrity QC and prove sacred blocks did not drift.
8. Run cannibalization + keyword-map + internal-link QC.
9. Run technical/live regression.
10. Only then mark **ADSENSE RESUBMISSION READY**.

## 10. Change-control requirement

Any content enhancement batch must record:

- sessions/languages touched;
- target search territory and reader intent;
- which allowed explanatory layers changed;
- explicit statement that sacred blocks were unchanged;
- owner/companion relationship check;
- before/after thin-content classification;
- sacred-integrity QC result;
- live deployment result.

**Core rule:** MONETIZATION MAY EXPAND USEFUL EXPLANATION. IT MAY NEVER REWRITE REVELATION OR VERIFIED HADITH SOURCE TEXT.

## 11. Muslim Daily-Life Utility + 700+ Original Depth Lock

TadabburLife is intended to become a **practical life companion for Muslims**, not a collection of generic religious articles. Every substantive session should start from a recognizable daily-life need that can be naturally illuminated by the session's Qur'an/hadith anchor.

Editorial question: **What real situation brings a Muslim to this guidance, what does the sacred anchor actually support, what should the reader reflect on, and what proportionate action can they take?**

When genuinely relevant to the sacred anchor, problem territories may include family, parenting, work and amanah, money/consumption, friendship, conflict, anger, envy, gratitude, patience, uncertainty, failure, success, loss, digital/social-media conduct, time/discipline, repentance/self-correction, and community responsibility. These are reader-problem territories—not permission to manufacture rulings.

Preferred reader-value layers are: (1) masalah nyata pembaca; (2) konteks petunjuk; (3) batas makna/interpretasi/prediksi, clearly separating revelation, sourced explanation, editorial synthesis and uncertainty; (4) muhasabah; (5) realistic practical action; (6) concrete daily-life examples; (7) relationship to previous/owner/next session; and (8) concise meaning-constrained life takeaway.

For the current editorial baseline, every **substantive canonical ID/EN session landing targets at least 700 words of original editorial/explanatory content per language**, excluding Qur'an Arabic, established Qur'an translation, quoted hadith, navigation/header/footer/CTA boilerplate, repeated legal copy, schema/metadata and duplicated template text.

**700+ is an internal TadabburLife quality floor, not a claim about a Google/AdSense minimum word count.** A page does not PASS merely by reaching 700 words. Padding, generic religious prose, repeated FAQ, paraphrase loops, keyword stuffing, mechanically expanded translation, unsupported religious claims and sacred-source drift are prohibited. If 700+ useful words cannot be written without stretching meaning or duplicating an owner session, do not inflate the page; reconsider its search intent, companion role or indexability.

For bilingual pages, both languages require independently useful editorial writing. English may adapt examples/structure to English search intent while preserving sacred-anchor and evidence-boundary parity.

A STRONG session should therefore satisfy: recognizable Muslim daily-life problem; standalone usefulness; clear source-vs-reflection boundary; explicit interpretation/prediction limits where needed; meaningful muhasabah; practical evidence-traceable action; unique contribution to the journey; 700+ original editorial words per language unless a documented role/indexability exception applies; and zero filler or sacred drift.

**Target experience:** SEARCH NEED → REAL-LIFE CONTEXT → SACRED GUIDANCE → BOUNDARY → MUHASABAH → PRACTICAL ACTION → CONNECTED JOURNEY.
