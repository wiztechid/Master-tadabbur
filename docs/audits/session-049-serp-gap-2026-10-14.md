## Editorial design
- Opening: what if advice triggers defensiveness? Distinguish counsel from humiliation.
- An-Nahl 16:125: wisdom, good exhortation, best manner of discussion; compare Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di with citations.
- Taha 20:44: Moses and Aaron instructed to speak gently to Pharaoh; avoid claiming identical contexts for every family disagreement.
- Ali Imran 3:159: Prophet's gentleness, forgiveness and consultation; distinguish leadership context from ordinary private counsel.
- Practical decision tree: verify facts and authority -> clarify intention -> choose private setting when appropriate -> ask permission/listen -> speak accurately and gently -> allow response -> pause when escalation is likely. Explicitly editorial application, not literal sequence commanded by these verses.
- Boundaries: serious harm, abuse, and legal duties may require protective action, not silence or secrecy; do not promise that kindness guarantees acceptance.
- Three concrete scenarios (family, friendship, workplace), muhasabah, one practical action, crosslinks to sessions 013 (3:134 / 41:34), 014 (17:53), 043 (31:19) without re-explaining their owned verses.
- Arabic text, accurate transliteration, sourced ID translation and public-domain EN translation; substantial bilingual tafsir and explicit references.
