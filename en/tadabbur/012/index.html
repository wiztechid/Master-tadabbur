Quran Verse About Even the Smallest Good Deed — Quran 99:7–8

SESSION 012 • AZ-ZALZALAH 99:7–8 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Do Not Belittle Small Acts of Good

Allah teaches that no deed is too small to fall outside accountability. Small acts of good deserve care, and small acts of wrong should not be dismissed.

🌙 Today's Key Verse

فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًۭا يَرَهُۥ ۝ وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍۢ شَرًّۭا يَرَهُۥ
Fa man ya‘mal miṡqāla żarratin khairan yarah. Wa man ya‘mal miṡqāla żarratin syarran yarah.
Az-Zalzalah 99:7–8 — And whoso doeth good an atom's weight will see it then, And whoso doeth ill an atom's weight will see it then.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are shared with the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

The closing two verses of Surah Az-Zalzalah reshape the way we judge what is “small.” A deed may look insignificant to people, yet no act—however small—falls outside Allah's knowledge and accountability.

Az-Zalzalah 99:7

Small acts of good still matter

This verse does not ask us to wait for a great opportunity before doing good. A simple act of help, a calming word, carefulness with a trust, or choosing not to hurt someone can all belong to the deeds that will be shown.

This does not mean every small deed automatically earns the same reward. The value of a deed is connected with faith, intention, sincerity, sound conduct, and Allah's acceptance. But small size is never a reason to dismiss it.

Az-Zalzalah 99:8

Small wrongs should not be normalized either

The paired verse keeps the balance. If small good should not be dismissed, small wrongs should not be treated as safe merely because they appear minor: a hurtful word, a small dishonesty, a delayed right, or negligence deliberately left uncorrected.

The aim is not obsessive anxiety. It is moral sensitivity: repeated small choices help shape habits and character.

📚 Tafsir Notes

Every deed will be shown

In Ibn Kathir's tafsir of Az-Zalzalah 99:7–8, these verses are understood in a very comprehensive way: good and evil, even when extremely small, will be shown to the one who did them. The verses nurture hope so that good is not wasted and caution so that wrongdoing is not belittled.

“Atom's weight” expresses extreme smallness, not a modern physics formula

The Qur'anic word żarrah emphasizes something extremely small. This reflection does not turn it into a claim that the Qur'an is teaching a specific measurement from modern particle physics. The moral message is clear without forcing that comparison: small scale does not remove an act from accountability.

📜 Relevant Hadith

Do not belittle any good deed

In Sahih Muslim 2626, Abu Dharr reports that the Prophet ﷺ told him not to consider any good deed insignificant, even meeting his brother with a cheerful face. The hadith gives a simple example: goodness does not need to be spectacular to be worth doing.

🌍 Fact × Lesson × Prediction Boundary

Life fact: relationships, workplace culture, and family atmosphere are often shaped by actions that appear small but are repeated—the way we respond, keep promises, clean up after ourselves, help without being asked, or hold back a hurtful remark.

Lesson/analogy: do not wait for a major project before becoming useful. Consistent small acts of good can help form good habits, while normalized small negligence can make room for larger negligence.

Prediction boundary: these verses affirm that deeds will be shown and accounted for, but they do not give us authority to assign a guaranteed amount of reward to a particular act or to determine another person's final destiny. Perfect judgment belongs to Allah.

💭 Reflection

What small thing have I been dismissing?

Is there one simple good deed I could realistically do every day but often postpone because it feels “too small”?

On the other side, is there a small habit I keep excusing because it seems insignificant, even though repeated choices are shaping who I become?

✨ Core Wisdom

Do not wait for a great opportunity to become good, and do not wait for a mistake to become great before stopping it. The direction of the soul is often shaped through small decisions repeated over time.

✅ Practical Action Today

Choose one small good deed that can be done in less than five minutes—help someone, tidy something, say thank you, contact a parent or family member, fulfill one small right, or hold back one unnecessary remark. Do it today without waiting for perfect conditions.
