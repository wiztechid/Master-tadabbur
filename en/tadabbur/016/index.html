Nightly Muhasabah: A Practical Self-Review Before Sleep

SESSION 016 • PRACTICAL CHECKPOINT • SEE SESSION 030

Audit Yourself Before the Day Ends

This session is a practical checkpoint for nightly self-review. Quran 59:18 and its tafsir are not repeated here; the primary verse discussion lives in Session 030.

🔗 Verse ownership

Al-Hashr 59:18 already has its primary discussion in Session 030 — What Have We Sent Ahead for Tomorrow?. This session does not repeat the verse text or its tafsir; use that page as the reference and continue with this session's distinct focus.

How should this nightly self-review checkpoint be used?

Use this session as a brief practice before sleep: note one blessing, one wrong or right that needs repair, and one good step to carry into the next day. For the verse and tafsir that anchor this practice, open Session 030.

🪞 Reflection

If today were a page I would see again before Allah, what part would I be most grateful for?

What one right, mistake, or good deed can I still address before this day truly ends?

💡 Wisdom

Do not only count what you gained today; also look at what you sent ahead for the Hereafter.

✅ Practical Action Today

Before sleep, make a three-line audit: Gratitude — one blessing consciously recognized; Istighfar — one wrong to repent from or repair; Provision — one small good deed you can still do or turn into a concrete next step.

🔗 Connection to the Previous Journey

Session 015 taught caution when judging others. Session 016 turns the mirror toward ourselves: before becoming busy auditing others, audit your own provision. Cross-reference Session 010 on time: time becomes valuable when it is directed toward what lasts.
