Characteristics of the Muttaqin in Quran 2:1–5

SESSION 002 • AL-BAQARAH 1–5

The Compass of the God-Conscious

Revelation, belief in the unseen, prayer, giving, continuity of revelation, and certainty in the Hereafter.

🌙 Today’s Key Verse

أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدًى مِّن رَّبِّهِمْ ۖ وَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ
Ulā’ika ‘alā hudam mir rabbihim, wa ulā’ika humul-mufliḥūn.
Al-Baqarah 2:5 — These depend on guidance from their Lord. These are the successful.

English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall. Arabic text and transliteration follow the published Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

Success does not end with worldly results. It begins with having the right direction in life.

Al-Baqarah 1–2

Revelation as a compass

الٓمٓ ۝ ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ
Alif Lām Mīm. Żālikal-kitābu lā raiba fīh, hudal lil-muttaqīn.
Alif. Lam. Mim. This is the Scripture whereof there is no doubt, a guidance unto those who ward off (evil).

The Qur’an is hudā—guidance. Guidance bears fruit in a heart willing to submit to truth.

Al-Baqarah 3

Faith → Worship → Social Responsibility

الَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْغَيْبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ
Allażīna yu’minūna bil-gaibi wa yuqīmūnaṣ-ṣalāta wa mimmā razaqnāhum yunfiqūn.
Who believe in the Unseen, and establish worship, and spend of that We have bestowed upon them.

Taqwa unites inner conviction, disciplined worship, and social responsibility.

Al-Baqarah 4

The horizon of the Hereafter

وَالَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِمَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْكَ وَمَا أُنزِلَ مِن قَبْلِكَ وَبِالْآخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوقِنُونَ
Wallażīna yu’minūna bimā unzila ilaika wa mā unzila min qablika wa bil-ākhirati hum yūqinūn.
And who believe in that which is revealed unto thee and that which was revealed before thee, and are certain of the Hereafter.

Certainty in the Hereafter lengthens the horizon of decisions: what is right is not always identical with what gives the fastest gain.

📚 Tafsir

One system of taqwa

The traits of the muttaqūn form one system: belief in the unseen → prayer → giving → receiving revelation → certainty in the Hereafter → living upon guidance.

📜 Hadith

The Hadith of Jibril

In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ explains iman, Islam, and ihsan. The hadith reinforces the bond between inward faith and outward action.

🌍 Fact × Lesson × Future Boundary

Fact: people inevitably make some decisions with incomplete information.

Lesson: faith sets the destination, prayer recalibrates direction, giving protects from selfishness, revelation becomes the map, and the Hereafter extends the horizon.

Future boundary: the Hereafter is part of faith; these verses are not a basis for setting a date for the Last Day or forcing modern events into specific signs.

💭 Reflection

If someone saw only my schedule, how I use money, and the decisions I make—would it be visible that I truly believe in Allah and the Hereafter?

✨ Practical Action Today

Review three signs from Al-Baqarah 3: the quality of one prayer, one decision made with awareness that Allah sees us, and one part of your provision shared with another person.

🔗 Connection to Session 001

Al-Fatihah 6 teaches us to pray, “Show us the straight path.” Al-Baqarah 2 then introduces the Qur’an as guidance for the God-conscious. After asking for guidance, we are introduced to its source.
