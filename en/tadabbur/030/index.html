Quran verse about preparing for the Hereafter

SESSION 030 • AL-HASHR 59:18 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Do Not Live Without Accounting for Tomorrow

Self-accounting, taqwa, future, responsibility

🌙 Today's Key Verse

يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ ٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَلْتَنظُرْ نَفْسٌۭ مَّا قَدَّمَتْ لِغَدٍۢ ۖ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرٌۢ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ
Yā ayyuhallażīna āmanuttaqullāha waltanẓur nafsum mā qaddamat ligad, wattaqullāh. Innallāha khabīrum bimā ta‘malūn.
AL-HASHR 59:18 — M. Pickthall: O ye who believe! Observe your duty to Allah. And let every soul look to that which it sendeth on before for the morrow. And observe your duty to Allah! Lo! Allah is Informed of what ye do.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are identical to the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

📚 Tafsir Notes

Self-accounting before the final account

Ibn Kathir explains that every soul should examine the deeds prepared for the Day of Resurrection. Repeated taqwa makes self-review part of consciousness of Allah, not merely productivity.

“Tomorrow” changes today

Al-Tabari understands what is sent ahead as deeds for the life after death, shifting attention from immediate satisfaction to long-term consequence.

Boundary

The verse primarily concerns the Hereafter. Applying its discipline to life or work planning is analogy, not prediction.

🤲 Authentic Hadith

Sahih al-Bukhari 6416: the Prophet ﷺ taught to live in this world like a stranger or traveller; Ibn ‘Umar then counselled against postponing deeds.

🌍 Factual Context

Fact: the verse commands taqwa and examination of what is sent ahead for “tomorrow”.

Lesson: reviewing decisions, time, responsibilities, and wealth can reveal life's direction.

Boundary: worldly planning is a means; the primary horizon is accountability before Allah.

🪞 Reflection

From the last seven days, what am I most clearly sending ahead?

Does my schedule reflect what I say matters most?

What wrong do I keep postponing?

💡 Main Wisdom

Self-accounting changes the future by examining what we plant today. Taqwa carries review toward accountability.

✅ Practical Action Today

Before sleeping, write one deed to be grateful for, one wrong to correct, and one concrete action for tomorrow.

🔗 Connection to the Journey

Session 029 emphasized striving. Session 030 adds a compass: do not merely move; examine where the steps lead.
