Quran 49:11: Mocking People and Offensive Names

SESSION 005 • AL-HUJURAT 49:11

Protecting the Honor of Others

Mockery, insults, and offensive labels can damage another person's dignity. This session focuses on Quran 49:11; suspicion, spying, and backbiting are handled separately in Session 015.

🔗 Verse ownership

Al-Hujurat 49:12 already has its primary discussion in Session 015 — Do Not Build a Verdict from Suspicion. This session does not repeat the verse text or its tafsir; use that page as the reference and continue with this session's distinct focus.

Al-Hujurat 11

🌙 Today's Key Verse

يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا يَسْخَرْ قَوْمٌۭ مِّن قَوْمٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونُوا۟ خَيْرًۭا مِّنْهُمْ وَلَا نِسَآءٌۭ مِّن نِّسَآءٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُنَّ خَيْرًۭا مِّنْهُنَّ ۖ وَلَا تَلْمِزُوٓا۟ أَنفُسَكُمْ وَلَا تَنَابَزُوا۟ بِٱلْأَلْقَـٰبِ ۖ بِئْسَ ٱلِٱسْمُ ٱلْفُسُوقُ بَعْدَ ٱلْإِيمَـٰنِ ۚ وَمَن لَّمْ يَتُبْ فَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ
Yā ayyuhallażīna āmanū, lā yaskhar qaumum min qaumin ‘asā ay yakūnū khairam minhum, wa lā nisā’um min nisā’in ‘asā ay yakunna khairam minhunna. Wa lā talmizū anfusakum wa lā tanābazū bil-alqāb. Bi’sal-ismul-fusūqu ba‘dal-īmān. Wa mal lam yatub fa ulā’ika humuẓ-ẓālimūn.
O ye who believe! Let not a folk deride a folk who may be better than they (are), nor let women (deride) women who may be better than they are; neither defame one another, nor insult one another by nicknames. Bad is the name of lewdness after faith. And whoso turneth not in repentance, such are evil-doers.

Allah breaks the illusion of superiority: the person being mocked may be better before Allah than the mocker. Human worth cannot be reduced to visible weaknesses, status, appearance, or one mistake from the past.

📚 Tafsir Mu’tabar

Al-Hujurat 11 — mockery and harmful labels

Ibn Kathir explains that “do not defame yourselves” includes defaming fellow believers, while the prohibition of insulting nicknames includes calling a person by a label they dislike. Honor must be protected even in ordinary speech.

📜 Authentic Hadith

Beware of suspicion

In Sahih al-Bukhari 6064 and 6066 and Sahih Muslim 2563a, the Prophet ﷺ warned against suspicion, prohibited searching for others’ faults and spying, and urged believers to be brothers.

What is backbiting?

In Sahih Muslim 2589, backbiting is explained as mentioning about your brother something he dislikes. If what is said is false, it becomes slander in addition to the harm already done.

🌍 Fact × Lesson × Prediction Boundary

Fact: group chats, screenshots, comments, and social platforms allow reputational harm to travel quickly and persist beyond the original conversation.

Lesson: not everything that is true needs to be circulated. Truthfulness, rightful purpose, benefit, and another person’s dignity all matter.

Boundary: these verses are moral guidance for human conduct; they are not predictions of social media or any specific modern technology.

💭 Reflection

When I hear negative information about someone, do I verify it, restrain myself, and protect their dignity—or do I enjoy passing it on?

When I joke, does the person being joked about feel comfortable too, or do I call it “just a joke” because I am not the one being hurt?

✨ Core Wisdom

Another person’s honor is also a trust. A believer does not merely avoid inventing lies; they also resist unnecessary circulation of another person’s weaknesses, faults, and humiliations.

✅ Practical Action Today

For one day, make sure your jokes and forms of address do not demean anyone: do not mock, insult, or use a nickname the person dislikes. If you do, stop and offer an appropriate apology.

🔗 Connection to Session 004

Session 004 treated rights and justice as amanah. Session 005 brings that principle into speech and social life: another person’s dignity is also a right that must not be taken merely because information is available.
