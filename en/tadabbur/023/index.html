Quran verse about returning trusts to their owners

SESSION 023 • AN-NISA 4:58 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Trusts Must Be Returned to Those Entitled to Them

A trust is not limited to property left in our care. It also reaches responsibility, authority, information, work, and rights entrusted to us. The Qur'an joins trust with justice: what is entrusted must reach those entitled to it, and decisions among people must be made justly.

🌙 Today's Key Verse

إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَن تُؤَدُّوا۟ ٱلْأَمَـٰنَـٰتِ إِلَىٰٓ أَهْلِهَا وَإِذَا حَكَمْتُم بَيْنَ ٱلنَّاسِ أَن تَحْكُمُوا۟ بِٱلْعَدْلِ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ نِعِمَّا يَعِظُكُم بِهِۦٓ ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًۭا
Innallāha ya’murukum an tu’addul-amānāti ilā ahlihā, wa iżā ḥakamtum bainan-nāsi an taḥkumū bil-‘adl. Innallāha ni‘immā ya‘iẓukum bih. Innallāha kāna samī‘am baṣīrā.
An-Nisa 4:58 — M. Pickthall: Lo! Allah commandeth you that ye restore deposits to their owners, and, if ye judge between mankind, that ye judge justly. Lo! comely is this which Allah admonisheth you. Lo! Allah is ever Hearer, Seer.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are identical to the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

Two commands stand together: deliver trusts to those entitled to them and exercise authority with justice. Trust and power are not private possessions to be used however we please.

📚 Tafsir Notes

Trusts include duties toward Allah and rights among people

Ibn Kathir explains that the command to render trusts includes the various trusts people are obliged to fulfill, both duties toward Allah and rights between people. The principle therefore reaches beyond physical deposits to responsibilities entrusted to a person.

Justice when exercising authority

Al-Tabari explains the command that those who decide matters among people must decide with justice. Holding decision-making authority does not remove one's duty toward truth and the rights of the parties concerned.

A broad principle with proper boundaries

The verse establishes a broad principle of trust and justice. Applying it to office, administration, employment, data, or resources is a lesson drawn from that principle; the legal and procedural details of each matter still depend on valid rules and the facts of the case.

🤲 Authentic Hadith

In Sahih al-Bukhari 33, the Prophet ﷺ identifies among the signs of hypocrisy that when a person is entrusted, he betrays the trust. Safeguarding trust is therefore a serious part of Muslim integrity, not merely an optional courtesy.

🌍 Factual Context

Fact from revelation: An-Nisa 4:58 explicitly commands returning trusts to those entitled to them and acting justly when deciding matters among people.

Lesson/analogy: in modern life, the principle of trust is relevant when thinking about entrusted money, office, data, system access, working time, organizational authority, and public service. The question is not only “Can I do this?” but also “Is this truly within my right and authority, and are those entitled to it being treated justly?”

Boundary: the verse does not by itself prescribe the design of modern government institutions or decide a particular administrative case. Applicable facts, rules, authority, evidence, and due process remain necessary. This is a revealed principle, not a prediction or verdict on a particular contemporary event.

🪞 Reflection

What trust is currently in my hands—money, work, information, time, authority, or someone's confidence?

Do I treat it as something entrusted to me and accountable before Allah, or have I begun to feel that because it is under my control, I may use it according to my own interests?

💡 Main Wisdom

The greater the trust placed in our hands, the stronger the reason to remember that it is not ours: trusts must reach those entitled to them, and authority must travel together with justice.

✅ Practical Action Today

Choose one trust currently under your control. Identify who is entitled to it, what your obligation is, and whether any part is overdue. Complete one concrete action today to fulfill it properly.
