Quran verse for someone who thinks their sins are too many

SESSION 031 • AZ-ZUMAR 39:53 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Do Not Despair of Allah's Mercy

Hope, repentance, mercy, returning to Allah

🌙 Today's Key Verse

قُلْ يَـٰعِبَادِىَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا۟ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا۟ مِن رَّحْمَةِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا ۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ
Qul yā ‘ibādiyallażīna asrafū ‘alā anfusihim lā taqnaṭū mir raḥmatillāh. Innallāha yagfiruż-żunūba jamī‘ā. Innahū huwal-gafūrur-raḥīm.
AZ-ZUMAR 39:53 — M. Pickthall: Say: O My slaves who have been prodigal to their own hurt! Despair not of the mercy of Allah, Who forgiveth all sins. Lo! He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are identical to the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

📚 Tafsir Notes

The door of return remains open

Ibn Kathir presents this as a call to sinners to return and repent. Vast forgiveness does not trivialize sin; it prevents guilt from becoming despair.

Forgiveness travels with return

The following verses command turning back and submitting to Allah. Vast forgiveness is therefore not permission to persist deliberately in sin without repentance.

Boundary

Repentance includes leaving sin, regret, resolve not to return, and where human rights are involved, proper restoration.

🤲 Authentic Hadith

Sahih Muslim 2747: Allah is more pleased with His servant's repentance than a person who unexpectedly recovers a lost mount after despairing of finding it.

🌍 Factual Context

Fact: the verse forbids despair of Allah's mercy and affirms broad forgiveness.

Lesson: healthy remorse moves toward correction; despair can drive a person away from repentance.

Boundary: forgiveness does not automatically erase victims' rights, restitution, or worldly legal consequences.

🪞 Reflection

Has a sin or failure made me feel too far gone to return?

Do I use Allah's mercy as strength to change or an excuse to delay?

If my wrong harmed another, what restoration remains undone?

💡 Main Wisdom

True hope does not trivialize sin; it refuses to surrender to sin. Do not close the door of return that Allah has opened.

✅ Practical Action Today

Choose one real wrong: stop it, seek forgiveness consciously, and if another person's right is involved, begin restoring it.

🔗 Connection to the Journey

Session 030 called for self-accounting. Session 031 answers when review reveals shortcomings: do not despair—return.
