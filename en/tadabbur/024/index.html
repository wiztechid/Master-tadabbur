Quran verse about fulfilling contracts and promises

SESSION 024 • AL-MA'IDAH 5:1 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Do Not Take Bound Commitments Lightly

Trust does not end with good intentions. Once a lawful promise, covenant, or commitment has been bound, the Qur'an teaches us to treat it as a responsibility to be fulfilled, not as words that may be discarded whenever circumstances change.

🌙 Today's Key Verse

يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ أَوْفُوا۟ بِٱلْعُقُودِ ۚ أُحِلَّتْ لَكُم بَهِيمَةُ ٱلْأَنْعَـٰمِ إِلَّا مَا يُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْكُمْ غَيْرَ مُحِلِّى ٱلصَّيْدِ وَأَنتُمْ حُرُمٌ ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحْكُمُ مَا يُرِيدُ
Yā ayyuhallażīna āmanū aufū bil-‘uqūd. Uḥillat lakum bahīmatul-an‘āmi illā mā yutlā ‘alaikum gaira muḥilliṣ-ṣaidi wa antum ḥurum. Innallāha yaḥkumu mā yurīd.
Al-Ma'idah 5:1 — M. Pickthall: O ye who believe! Fulfil your indentures. The beast of cattle is made lawful unto you (for food) except that which is announced unto you (herein), game being unlawful when ye are on the pilgrimage. Lo! Allah ordaineth that which pleaseth Him.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are identical to the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

The surah opens with a direct command: fulfill the bonds you have entered. Faith has consequences for how a person treats commitments, including when fulfillment requires discipline and consistency.

📚 Tafsir Notes

Covenants are broader than commercial contracts

Ibn Kathir explains al-‘uqūd in terms of bonds and covenants that must be fulfilled, encompassing different legitimate commitments. Its meaning is broader than buying and selling alone: a believer is expected to take binding responsibilities seriously.

The command is to fulfill, not to hunt for loopholes

Al-Tabari explains the command as requiring fulfillment of covenants and bonds that Allah has ordered people to observe. Its reflective lesson shifts the question from merely “What can I escape?” toward “What has become my responsibility?”

Boundary of application

Not every human promise can make the forbidden lawful or override revealed limits. The verse establishes the seriousness of lawful covenants; details concerning rights, conditions, termination, and disputes still depend on the rules governing the particular covenant.

🤲 Authentic Hadith

In Sahih al-Bukhari 33, the Prophet ﷺ identifies among the signs of hypocrisy that when a person makes a promise, he breaks it. The hadith reinforces the lesson that consistency between words and actions is an important part of Muslim integrity.

🌍 Factual Context

Fact from revelation: Al-Ma'idah 5:1 explicitly commands believers to fulfill covenants.

Lesson/analogy: this principle is relevant to commitments in family life, employment, transactions, contracts, service targets, and organizational responsibilities. A document or statement that has created an obligation should not be treated as a mere formality.

Boundary: the verse does not automatically decide the validity of a particular modern contract. A concrete case still requires examination of the agreement, applicable law, the authority of the parties, and relevant facts. No claim of predicting modern events is being made here.

🪞 Reflection

Do I distinguish a truly binding commitment from a passing intention I never promised?

Is there a promise or commitment I once made easily but now treat as though it never existed?

When fulfilling a commitment becomes difficult, do I seek the right way to resolve it—or merely search for a reason to escape it?

💡 Main Wisdom

Integrity becomes clearest when fulfillment costs time, effort, or humility. A lawful covenant does not lose its weight merely because circumstances change.

Integrity becomes visible when our words retain their weight after circumstances change. A believer does not turn promises into decoration, but treats them as responsibilities to be guarded.

✅ Practical Action Today

Revisit one unfinished commitment—to family, work, an organization, or another person. Identify one concrete step you can complete today, and do it before adding another promise.
