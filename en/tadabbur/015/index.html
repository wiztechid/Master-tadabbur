SESSION 015 • AL-HUJURAT 49:12 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Do Not Build a Verdict from Suspicion

An unproven assumption can grow into searching for faults and then into speaking about another person's honor. The Qur'an breaks that chain near its beginning: do not let baseless suspicion grow into spying and backbiting.

🌙 Today's Key Verse

يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ ٱجْتَنِبُوا۟ كَثِيرًۭا مِّنَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِنَّ بَعْضَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِثْمٌۭ ۖ وَلَا تَجَسَّسُوا۟ وَلَا يَغْتَب بَّعْضُكُم بَعْضًا ۚ أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمْ أَن يَأْكُلَ لَحْمَ أَخِيهِ مَيْتًۭا فَكَرِهْتُمُوهُ ۚ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ
Yā ayyuhallażīna āmanujtanibū kaṡīran minaẓ-ẓanni inna ba‘ḍaẓ-ẓanni iṡmun, wa lā tajassasū wa lā yaghtab ba‘ḍukum ba‘ḍā. A yuḥibbu aḥadukum an ya'kula laḥma akhīhi maytan fa karihtumūh. Wattaqullāh, innallāha tawwābur-raḥīm.
Al-Hujurat 49:12 — M. Pickthall: O ye who believe! Shun much suspicion; for lo! some suspicion is a crime. And spy not, neither backbite one another. Would one of you love to eat the flesh of his dead brother? Ye abhor that (so abhor the other)! And keep your duty (to Allah). Lo! Allah is Relenting, Merciful.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are shared with the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

The verse does not say that every suspicion is sinful. It warns against suspicion without sound basis being treated as established fact. That distinction matters when reading messages, judging colleagues, understanding family members, or reacting to fragments of information.

Al-Hujurat 49:12

An assumption is not a fact

Ibn Kathir explains the prohibition of suspicion that has no proper foundation. As long as a reasonable good explanation remains possible, a believer should not rush from ambiguity to accusation.

This is not a call to be naive. Caution remains appropriate when evidence, risk, a duty of review, or responsibility to protect others is present. What must be resisted is turning unsupported suspicion into a moral verdict.

📚 Tafsir Notes

Suspicion can open the door to social sins

Ibn Kathir places the prohibition of unfounded suspicion alongside the prohibitions of spying and backbiting. The sequence offers a powerful lesson: a suspicious interpretation can push a person to search for faults and then spread them.

Tajassus is not ordinary responsible inquiry

The prohibition of tajassus protects dignity and privacy. Not every investigation is forbidden spying; legitimate review needed to prevent harm, fulfill a trust, conduct an audit, enforce rules, or protect rights belongs to a different context. Personal curiosity, however, is not a license to expose faults.

Backbiting can be factually true and still be backbiting

In the hadith explanation cited by Ibn Kathir, backbiting is mentioning about your brother what he dislikes. If what is said is actually true, it is backbiting; if it is false, it becomes a false accusation.

🤲 Hadith

In Sahih Muslim 2563a, the Prophet ﷺ warns against suspicion, inquisitively searching into one another, spying, envy, malice, hostility, and turning away from one another, then calls Allah's servants to live as brothers.

The hadith shows that healthy relationships are protected not only at the level of outward action. They are also protected in how we interpret other people.

🧭 Fact • Lesson • Boundary

Fact from revelation: Al-Hujurat 49:12 prohibits much suspicion, spying, and backbiting; authentic hadith likewise warns against suspicion and spying.

Lesson: do not promote an assumption to the status of fact simply because it feels plausible. Separate what is actually known, what is merely interpreted, and what still requires verification.

Boundary: this verse does not cancel verification, audits, legitimate professional review, lawful investigation, risk awareness, or the duty to report wrongdoing. Thinking well of others does not mean closing one's eyes to evidence. No prediction about a modern event is drawn from this verse.

🪞 Reflection

Is there someone I have recently judged mainly from an assumption rather than from facts I genuinely know?

Have I ever searched for more information not to solve a problem, but to confirm a suspicion I had already decided to believe?

💡 Wisdom

Do not give an assumption the status that belongs only to a fact.

✅ Practical Action Today

When one suspicion about someone appears today, make three simple columns in your mind or on paper: what I know, what I assume, and what I need to verify. Do not pass the assumption to others unless there is a legitimate need and adequate basis.
