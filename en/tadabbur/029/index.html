Quran 29:69: Those Who Strive for Allah Are Guided

SESSION 029 • AL-ANKABUT 29:69 • THEMATIC/NON-LINEAR

Guidance Is Met with Striving

Striving, guidance, effort, excellence.

Quick answer: what does Quran 29:69 mean by “those who strive for Us”?

Quran 29:69 promises guidance to those who strive for Allah and affirms that Allah is with the doers of good. It is not a guarantee that hard work will produce every worldly goal; its focus is striving toward Allah and being guided to paths of good.

🌙 Today's Key Verse

وَٱلَّذِينَ جَـٰهَدُوا۟ فِينَا لَنَهْدِيَنَّهُمْ سُبُلَنَا ۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمَعَ ٱلْمُحْسِنِينَ
Wallażīna jāhadū fīnā lanahdiyannahum subulanā. Wa innallāha lama‘al-muḥsinīn.
AL-ANKABUT 29:69 — M. Pickthall: As for those who strive in Us, We surely guide them to Our paths, and lo! Allah is with the good.
English Qur'an translation reference: M. Pickthall (public-domain translation). Arabic text and transliteration are identical to the Indonesian edition. Transliteration is a reading aid and does not replace tajwid or talaqqi.

📚 Tafsir Notes

Striving in obedience

Ibn Kathir explains that those who strive in obedience are guided toward paths of good. Sound effort meets Allah's help and guidance.

Orientation remains toward Allah

The phrase fīnā preserves direction: not pursuit of any ambition, but effort for Allah within ways pleasing to Him.

Boundary

The verse does not guarantee that every worldly target pursued with hard work must be achieved. Guidance is not identical with a particular material success.

🤲 Authentic Hadith

Sahih Muslim 2664: pursue what benefits you, seek Allah's help, and do not give in to helplessness.

🌍 Factual Context

Fact from revelation: the verse promises guidance to those who strive in Allah's cause.

Lesson/analogy: intentions in learning, work, or changing habits need consistent action while methods are still evaluated.

Boundary: the verse does not guarantee promotion, wealth, project success, or a particular worldly outcome.

🪞 Reflection

Do I truly lack guidance, or do I already know the first step but have not striven?

Is my goal still within a path pleasing to Allah?

When results remain unseen, do I improve my effort while seeking Allah's help?

💡 Main Wisdom

Guidance does not make effort unnecessary. Striving toward Allah forms a person who keeps moving, learning, and correcting methods without making faith depend on instant results.

✅ Practical Action Today

Choose one good pursuit you have delayed. Work on its first step for at least ten minutes today, then continue consistently.
